# App shell (index.html)

Scope: the whole app, four sections on one page. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the owner, on a Xiaomi Pad 7 in landscape, evenings, lights low, pen in hand. Log today's habits in one tap each, see the robot respond, spend earnings in the shop, handwrite a diary page.

Constraints: landscape tablet first (about 1280 to 1600 CSS px wide, 3:2). Touch targets 44px or more. Works offline. Brief pins: clean and futuristic, robot theme present, animated WALL-E/EVE-style robot from the owner's sketch, bottom category bar, a true diary template on matte black or cream, no grid paper.

Build path: code-led (no image generation in this session).

Unresolved: app name "Nuts & Bolts" and robot name "Rivet" are authored placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A robot's charging bay at night, kept by someone who sorts fasteners. Every habit is a hex nut the owner tightens. Refuses the category default of a black dashboard with neon glow edges and a grid of stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep blue-black ink ground with a sparse star field and one faint atom (the owner rejected the notebook grid). A saturated cobalt field (the bay) owns the left region of every screen and always holds the robot. Ceramic white for the robot shell and primary text. Zinc silver for bolts, brass for nuts. Mint signal for anything pressable-as-primary, for done, and for the robot's eyes. Coral only for failed. Oxanium (chamfered, self-hosted) for display, labels and numerals; platform sans for body. Rows and hairlines, not cards. Every part carries a catalog number.

STORY: The owner sees the robot and today's fasteners, tightens them, watches the robot cheer and the wallet tick, then spends in the shop or writes the day's page.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Base, 1600x1068): top strip with wordmark, date, and bolt and nut balances at right. Left 60 percent: cobalt bay, robot about 680px tall on a round charging platform, nameplate and mood at top left of the bay. Right 40 percent on ink: Today (each habit with its hex slots, button to Habits), Attributes (four segmented bars), This week (seven dots sized by completion). Bottom: four-tab category bar, Base, Shop, Habits, Diary.

SIGNATURE: hex fastener slots. Done turns the nut a sixth and fills it mint, skipped stays a hollow dashed nut, failed goes coral with a stripped cross. The same mark appears on Base, Habits and the calendar.

Raises, by donor: variable-font specimen (hierarchy by scale contrast, not boxes); factory catalog sleeve (catalog numbers on every part); star atlas (calendar days as dots on a fixed size ramp); warm consumer surface (saturated colour only where something can be pressed, generous targets).

FORM: fastener bin catalog, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list, materials translated into the brief's pinned clean futuristic robot world. Seed key 03176c0c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
