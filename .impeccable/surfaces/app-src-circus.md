# Five Nights at the Circus (circus flavour)
Scope: the whole app in its circus flavour (app/src/circus), the owner's partner's phone. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: the owner's girlfriend, on her Android phone at night; same jobs as the workshop app (log habits beside the animatronic, spend in the Prize Corner, plan acts, handwrite the night log). EN, PT-PT, RU.
Constraints: every screen, layout and function shared with main; only Skin.kt, Inks.kt, Parts.kt, Art.kt, icons, sounds, launcher icon, name and the word overlay change. Diary inks and papers stay owner-pinned. Original art inspired by FNAF (classic and withered animatronics) and Sister Location's circus; no ripped game assets or audio.
Build path: code-led.
## Direction contract
THESIS: Each habit is a marquee sign over the circus ring; every Done lights a bulb, and a fully lit sign chases its lights while the animatronic performs. Refuses the dark dashboard of glow rings.
OWN-WORLD: A big top after close. Plum-black ground #120A14, cards #1D1020; bulb amber #FFC94A the only light (bulbs, spotlight, gauges, Done); canvas cream #F3E6D3 text; tent red #7A1426 as material only (stripes, ring box); Faz-Token pink #FF7AAE only for tokens; coral #FF5C5C only for a smashed bulb. Rye display, Manrope body. Slots are bulbs: lit, dark, unscrewed (skip), smashed (fail). Raise (Saville): the room falls to near-black outside one spotlight.
